# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- **In your language**: German, French, Spanish, Russian and Brazilian Portuguese game clients now show the addon in their language (other clients stay in English). Russian clients get the game's own font for headings, since the addon's fonts have no Cyrillic letters.
- Quests now also record the currencies they reward (like the Merchant's Favor a crate turn-in gives), so Tylesser - Commerce Authority can show crate Favor from players' data.

## 1.0.0

- First release. Records the WoW Forever game data you see while you play (NPCs, quests, drops, gathering, rares and
  treasures, vendors, trainers, travel, places, recipes, auction prices) and never anything about players.
- `/tcollect` window: every kind of data with its count, a searchable list, the details of each entry, Show on map.
- Upload: the link to tylesser.com/collect and where the file is; your newest upload replaces your older ones.
